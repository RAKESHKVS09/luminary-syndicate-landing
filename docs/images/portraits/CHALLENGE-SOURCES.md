# Example community challenges

These concise labels express possibilities for people to collaborate on community questions. They do not describe existing Luminary projects or imply that a region faces only one problem. Stock photograph positions are conceptual and are separate from verified photography locations.

| Region | Website label | Primary supporting source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| North America | Food access | [FAO, The State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World 2025, Chapter 2](https://www.fao.org/fileadmin/templates/cfs/CFS53/Plenary_documents/Inf.15_SOFI_Report/EN_compiled.pdf) discusses food insecurity in Northern America. |
| Latin America | Restoring nature | [UNEP regional ecosystem restoration action plan](https://wedocs.unep.org/handle/20.500.11822/34950) supports protection and restoration of terrestrial, inland water and marine ecosystems. |
| Europe | Climate resilience | [UNECE and UNDRR, European urban resilience examples](https://w3.unece.org/sdg2024/story-9.html) describe local responses to heat and wildfire risk. |
| Africa | Safe water | [WHO African Region, water and sanitation report](https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2024-08/WHO_10yearTMAreport.pdf) describes remaining water access and community WASH gaps. |
| Asia Pacific | Ocean health | [UNEP COBSEA marine pollution prevention](https://www.unep.org/cobsea/what-we-do/marine-pollution-prevention-reduction-and-control) addresses plastic leakage and marine litter in the East Asian Seas. |
| South Asia | Learning access | [UNICEF regional education programme](https://www.unicef.org/asia-pacific/what-we-do/education) explicitly discusses South Asia learning and education access gaps. [UNICEF South Asia education quality report](https://www.unicef.org/rosa/media/6991/file/Improving%20Education%20Quality%20in%20South%20Asia%20%28I%29_30Aug_019_Web.pdf.pdf) provides additional regional context. |

Sources reviewed on 13 October 2026. No statistics or claim of regional exclusivity are used on the website. Photographs illustrate people and shared action without assigning hardship, nationality or membership to a photographed person. The central star is a code rendered optical light animation rather than a photograph of a celestial object.
