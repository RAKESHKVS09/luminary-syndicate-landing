# World scene photography

The opening and shared world scenes use actual licensed photographs of people, planting, water access, litter collection and shared learning. No photographic face or community scene was generated. Portraits and community action frames are illustrative stock photography and do not identify Luminary members or imply endorsements. Regional labels name example challenges. They do not establish where a picture was taken, a subject's nationality or a current Luminary project.

All photographs use the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/). Five displayed new images are listed below. The sixth scene uses the previously credited learning photograph by Ivan S, Pexels 5676744, from `../people-study.jpg`. Local photographs were downloaded through Pexels' image service as square JPEGs. Keep this record and the visible Image credits when integrating the page.
